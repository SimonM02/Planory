### Description (max. 4000 chars)
```
Planory is your digital construction assistant for private building projects – whether you're building a house, renovating or remodelling. Keep budget, costs and deadlines under control, without paper chaos and ten spreadsheets.

WHAT PLANORY DOES

• Budget & costs: Plan your total budget, split it into categories and instantly see what has been spent and what's left.
• Scan invoices: Upload a photo or PDF – the AI reads amount, date and supplier automatically and books the expense.
• AI construction advisor: Analyses your project, warns about cost traps and gives concrete recommendations for the next steps.
• Compare quotes: Record quotes, compare them side by side and turn one into an invoice with a single click.
• Site diary & photos: Document progress with notes and photos – sorted into albums and building phases.
• Schedule & tasks: Keep milestones and to-dos in view, with reminders for important dates.
• Maintenance plan: Never miss a service for heating, water or electrics again.
• Pipes & wiring: Record where pipes and wiring run – with photos and plans, so you're safe the next time you drill.
• Contacts & tradespeople: All the people involved in your project in one place.

WHO IS IT FOR?

For everyone building, renovating or remodelling a home who wants to keep their costs under control – simple and easy to understand, no construction-software degree required.

EVERYWHERE WITH YOU

Planory is available as an app and on the web (planory.at). Your data stays in sync across all devices and is stored securely.

PRICING

Try it free for one month. After that you can still view your data; new entries and the AI features require a fair subscription.
```
